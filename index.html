For parents

Tap a game. A voice asks for a picture. Any tap is fine: the picture that was tapped is named slowly in English, then Hebrew and Thai (switch them with the buttons at the top). There are no timers, no scores, and no losing screens. Each round is three finds and ends with a cheer.

Hebrew and Thai are spoken only if this device has a Hebrew or Thai voice installed (iPad: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices). Please have the Thai words checked first; see thai-review.md.
